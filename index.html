Who we are

Engineering excellence that transforms communities

Thomani Consulting was established in 2020 by a professional who is passionate about delivering engineering excellence, effective project delivery, and sustainable infrastructure solutions. The company was founded on the principles of professionalism, integrity, innovation, and a commitment to improving the quality of life within the communities we serve.

Thomani Consulting is a proudly South African, 100% Black-owned multidisciplinary engineering and construction management company providing professional services across the built environment. We specialise in Consulting Engineering, Construction Management, Contracts Management, Project Management, and Infrastructure Advisory Services, delivering practical and innovative solutions from project inception through to successful completion.

Our team comprises experienced Professional Construction Managers, Construction Project Managers, Engineering Technologists, and technical support personnel who possess extensive experience across both the public and private sectors. The professionals are registered with the South African Council for the Project and Construction Management Professions (SACPCMP) and the Engineering Council of South Africa (ECSA). We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of professional integrity while delivering quality, cost-effective, and sustainable infrastructure solutions aligned with industry best practices.

Pr CM Or Pr CPM · Criteria

Registration Criteria for Pr CM and Pr CPM

An applicant who wishes to apply for registration as Pr CM and Pr CPM shall possess at a minimum the following:

a) Qualification requirements

  • BSc Hons Construction Management NQF Level 8, 480 Credits
  • Post-graduate Diploma in Construction Management (NQF Level 8, 480 Credits)
  • Bachelors of the Built Environment Honours in Construction Studies (NQF Level 8, 480 Credits)
  • BSc Hons in Construction Project Management (NQF Level 8, 480 Credits)
  • RPL Certificate

Status: SACPCMP Accredited (including accredited and phased out HEQSF non-aligned qualifications obtained before June 2024)

An Equivalent Built Environment Qualification on the Recognition List (Annexure A) at NQF Level 8, 480 Credits

Status: Subjected to educational evaluation

b) Years of experience requirement

Minimum of five (5) years relevant practical experience

1st Criteria Non-Candidate Route

Honours NQF L8 — minimum 5 years post-qualification project management experience is required.
